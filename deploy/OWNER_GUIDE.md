# Munim — owner quick guide

**Start with Setup.** Enter your business profile and GST details, import your catalogue,
upload FAQs and policies, then choose tone, languages and hours. Test a known price in
the playground and check its citation before copying the widget snippet to your website.
Customers see a consent notice before starting a web chat. WhatsApp setup is separate;
ask your operator to verify the Meta test flow before connecting customers.

**Keep prices and answers current.** Edit the catalogue and pricing rules when rates
change. Munim calculates quotation amounts from those settings. Add missing policies
through Knowledge and review its knowledge gaps; unsupported answers should be referred
to your team. A preview in the playground helps you verify each change.

**Review your Inbox.** Open a conversation to see messages, citations and pending work.
Use **Take over** when you want to reply yourself. Assist mode prepares a private draft
for your approval; return to AI when the issue is resolved. If a customer complains or
asks for a person, check the handoff notification promptly.

**Approve quotations and follow-ups.** Check customer requirements, quantities, GST,
validity and discounts before approving a quote. Review follow-up drafts before release.
WhatsApp sending depends on its service window, approved templates and customer consent.
An uncertain delivery needs reconciliation; ask your operator before resending.

**Track leads and learn.** Update lead stages and lost reasons. Overview shows enquiry
trends and quotation status; Ask Munim can answer “Who hasn't responded this week?”
or “How many quotes were accepted?” Review Monday's weekly digest. Empty metrics mean
there is not enough recorded activity yet. Track your time saved alongside the dashboard.

**Handle customer privacy requests.** Verify the requester and your retention obligations.
In **Privacy**, find the customer, select the record and confirm your review before
erasing it. Erasure removes linked conversations, quotations and files; retry pending
file cleanup if shown. Ask your operator to handle provider-held data, exports and backups
separately. Keep passwords, API tokens and sensitive customer information out of chat.
