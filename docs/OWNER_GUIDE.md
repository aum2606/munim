# Owner guide (one page)

Munim answers your customers on WhatsApp from **your** catalogue and FAQs. It never makes up a price.
You stay in charge: nothing with a price reaches a customer until you approve it, and you can take over
any chat.

## Set up (about 30 minutes)

1. **Business profile** (Settings): your name, GSTIN, address, phone and the terms printed on quotations.
   The GSTIN decides CGST + SGST or IGST.
2. **Catalogue** (Knowledge, Catalogue): upload an Excel or CSV of what you sell, with prices. A template
   is provided. Check the preview, fix any row marked as an error, then save. **These prices are the only
   prices the assistant will ever quote.**
3. **FAQs and documents** (Knowledge): add your hours, delivery, payment terms, minimum order, address.
   One question and one answer each. You can also upload a PDF or Word price list or policy.
4. **Try it** (Playground): chat as a customer in English, Hindi, Gujarati or Hinglish. Tap a reply to
   see how it was made. Fix anything wrong by correcting the catalogue or FAQ, not by arguing with it.
5. **Connect WhatsApp** (Settings, WhatsApp): paste the phone number ID and access token from your Meta
   app. Whoever hosts this for you sets the webhook (see the self-host guide).

## Every day

- **Inbox**: all chats. **Take over** to reply yourself (the assistant goes silent); **Hand back to AI**
  when you are done. Complaints, requests to talk to a person, price haggling and order-status questions
  are handed to you automatically and show as "Needs a person".
- **Quotes**: when a customer asks for a quotation, the assistant collects the details and prepares one.
  Open it, set a price on any line marked "Set a price", change anything, then **Approve and send**. The
  customer receives the PDF on WhatsApp. When they accept, mark it accepted.
- **Knowledge, Unanswered questions**: things customers asked that it could not answer. Answer one and it
  becomes an FAQ, so it will know next time.
- **Follow-ups**: quotations nobody answered, with a suggested message. WhatsApp only lets you message
  customers for 24 hours after they last wrote, so after that you copy the text and send it from your phone.
- **Metrics**: messages, replies, leads, quotations, how fast it replies, and what the AI costs.

## Good to know

- Voice notes are turned into text. If it is not sure what it heard, it repeats it back and asks the
  customer to confirm.
- A customer who sends a flood of messages is not answered past a limit; you can still read them.
- To see or delete one customer's data, ask whoever runs the system (it is in the privacy notice).
- Free AI plans are slow and have limits. If the assistant cannot reach a model, it hands the chat to you
  rather than guessing.
